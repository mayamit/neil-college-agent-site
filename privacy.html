Privacy Policy

Neil College Agent is a private app used by one family. It accesses a single Google account (Gmail, Google Drive and Google Sheets) belonging to that family, only to organize college application materials.

Data accessed by the app is used solely by the family. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
